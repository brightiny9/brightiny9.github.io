I write about what we're building, and I pick similar products and companies to look at how they were built, how they make money, and how they differ. I dig through public sources, and when there are numbers, I talk in numbers.

## What you can expect

- When I write about our own product, I'll always say so.
- Sources, always.
- No ads or sponsored posts. If that ever changes, I'll say so.
- If I get something wrong, I'll fix it and note the correction.
- No visitor tracking.
