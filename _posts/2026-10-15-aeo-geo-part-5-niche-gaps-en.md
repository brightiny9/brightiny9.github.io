## Our math

These are our calculations from the list prices above.

**1. Count by price band: zero under $10, three between $10 and $29, two near $49, one at $100.** PromptScout, Rankscale and OtterlyAI sit at $15 to $29. LLM Pulse and Dageno start around $49. Trakkr starts at $100. Nothing below $15. So the cheapest tier doesn't fade toward zero. It stops at a floor. (Mixing € and $ makes the $49 band approximate.)

**2. The floor comes from plan size, not from the cost of a check.** Take OtterlyAI Lite: $29 for 15 prompts, checked daily on four engines. That's 15 × 4 × 30 = 1,800 prompt-engine checks a month, or about $0.016 each. Now picture a solo maker who tracks 5 questions on those same four engines daily: 5 × 4 × 30 = 600 checks. At Lite's own per-check rate, that's about $9.67. They'd pay $29, which is roughly 3 times more, for capacity they use a third of. PromptScout Pro tells the same story at the daily tier: $149 for 40 prompts on five engines is 40 × 5 × 30 = 6,000 checks, about $0.025 each. Five prompts daily is 750 of those, or $18.75 at that rate, against $149. (Both rates assume every listed engine is checked on every prompt, which the pages imply but don't spell out.)

**3. Weekly is the cheap tier, and weekly may be enough or not.** PromptScout Starter's 15 prompts across five engines weekly come to about 15 × 5 × 4.3 = 325 checks, around $0.046 each. That's the only sub-$20 option, and its price comes from checking less often. We can't tell you whether a weekly look is enough, because AI answers vary from run to run and we haven't tested how much.

## What this tells us, and what it doesn't

The six pages support one claim: a daily, multi-engine tracker for a handful of prompts isn't sold for under $10 anywhere we could read. The per-check math suggests such a plan could exist without losing money on checks, but we don't know the vendors' costs, support load or churn, so this is a hypothesis, not a finding.

Three other gaps came up in our earlier notes. We hold them with less confidence:

- **Korean-language and Naver AI results.** On October 11 we found one agency (about ₩5M a month with a six-month minimum, per its page; we haven't rechecked it) and a scraper API, but no self-serve tracker. Weak evidence: we read few pages and can't speak for what we missed.
- **"Register once" hosting.** Pieces exist: schema generators, llms.txt tools, brand-facts plugins. We found no single service that hosts a product page, structured data and llms.txt together. Low confidence; some pages weren't readable.
- **Getting cited where AI looks.** Crowded, by our read: directory-submission services at $1 to $299. Not a gap.

## The part that isn't a gap

A price hole isn't proof of demand. In part 1, the only Stripe-verified revenue we found came from an agency and two tools whose average customer pays around $220 a month. We found no verified evidence of solo makers paying for tracking at all. The $10 gap may exist because few people want it, not because nobody noticed. We're trying to find out by asking makers directly, and so far we have no answers to report.

If you track AI answers yourself today, the free route is your own: ask the same five questions once a week and log the answers in a spreadsheet. That's our opinion, not a tested method.
